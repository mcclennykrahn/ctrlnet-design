# Denominator table scroll affordance

Candidate `thesis_denom_scroll_affordance` gives the result table a named keyboard-focusable scroll region, a visible focus ring, and a pinned line-number column. A hint appears in result panels up to 280px wide. At 320px viewport width the table has 242px of space for 281px of content; users can scroll to the tolerance column while retaining the line number. The wrapper already fitted the viewport before this change. This improves navigation and visual orientation, rather than removing the need to scroll. The hint adds vertical space on narrow panels.

Evidence is recorded at `/workspace/ctrlnet-v08/cn_frontend_visual_denom_scroll/`: baseline source, before/after Chromium screenshots at 1440×950, 900×950, 390×844, 320×568 and 740×360, executable focused harness, source patch, full pytest log, browser regression logs and independent review. After captures show the focused table scrolled to its right edge; before captures show its initial position, so they are not pixel-aligned comparisons.
