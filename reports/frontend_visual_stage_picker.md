# Thesis view picker

The thesis page places the view picker above the project commands. Its full-width row gives the existing native selector a 44px target and a distinct label. This uses additional vertical space in the sidebar.

Chromium evidence covers desktop, tablet, and phone widths at 1440, 900, 390, and 320px. The focused check exercises all five views, URL updates, and a direct link. The thesis, shared-project and GNSS, and robust calculation browser regressions also passed.

Evidence directory and source patch are under `/workspace/ctrlnet-v08/cn_frontend_visual_stage_picker`.
