# Thesis calculation summary

Candidate `thesis_result_summary` makes the precision verdict and weakest
point easier to scan. The metric cards use two columns in narrow result panels
and four in wider panels. The layout follows the panel's available width.
Long values can wrap within their cards.

Before and after Chromium captures at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px,
along with the focused check, pytest, browser regressions and independent
review, are under `/workspace/ctrlnet-v08/cn_frontend_visual_thesis_results/`.
